# JAMS Coding Study

A browser-based coding application for a study of humour and empowerment in AI-related social-media communication.

## Contribution

The repository operationalises an interpretive research design as a structured annotation workflow. Human coders work through distributed sessions and export their decisions for analysis.

**AI as a research phenomenon:** the study investigates how AI is framed in public communication and how humour relates to perceived human empowerment.

**AI as methodology:** structured human annotation supplies transparent, inspectable evidence for computational and mixed-methods research.

**Teaching and impact:** suitable as a case for training in coding schemes, platform-data research, intercoder reflection and ethical interpretation. Findings can inform practitioners who communicate about AI and its human consequences.

The repository contains the study interface and coding sessions.
